Diploma

🎓MSBTE Diploma in Computer Engineering is a technical diploma course offered by the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE). It provides practical and theoretical knowledge about computer hardware, software, programming, and networking. The course typically lasts for three years and prepares students for careers in IT and computer-related industries. It focuses on skills like programming languages, database management, and computer system design. After completing this diploma, students can either start working in the industry or pursue higher education like a B.Tech degree.
